# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TableSpark welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, learning needs, and perspectives. Project spaces should remain constructive, safe, and focused on improving the software and its documentation.

Expected behavior includes:

- communicating with patience and respect;
- giving actionable technical feedback rather than personal criticism;
- respecting accessibility needs and different learning contexts;
- accepting correction and updating inaccurate claims;
- protecting personal information shared accidentally;
- keeping disagreement focused on ideas, evidence, and project tradeoffs.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, or targeted humiliation;
- sexualized or otherwise inappropriate conduct;
- publishing another person’s private information;
- deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith engagement;
- pressuring contributors to disclose personal details;
- using project spaces to distribute secrets, stolen data, or malicious payloads.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release coordination, and other official TableSpark project spaces.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. If the report involves a security vulnerability, use the process in `SECURITY.md` instead.

Reports should include enough context to understand the incident without unnecessarily repeating private or harmful content. Maintainers may remove content, lock discussions, warn participants, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project community.

## Enforcement principles

Responses should be proportionate, consistent, and focused on restoring a productive environment. Good-faith mistakes should generally be handled with clarification before escalation, while serious threats, harassment, or privacy violations may require immediate action.
